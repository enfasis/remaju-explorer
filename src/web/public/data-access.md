# Data access

Published data lives at <https://data.remaju.mejora.me/>. Start from [data/manifest.json](https://data.remaju.mejora.me/data/manifest.json): it gives the current `dataset_version`, its `scope`, and the `availability`, `state` and `archive` paths, all relative to that host. Releases under `data/releases/<run_id>/` never change once published. These files are public and can be fetched without JavaScript or an API key.

A manifest with `"scope": "partial"` comes from a collection limited to a few listing pages of REMAJU. It is not complete and does not establish current availability. Only `"scope": "all-phases"` releases come from a full walk of the listing. Local development and the offline sample use [demo/manifest.json](demo/manifest.json) on the site itself; its `demo` field identifies it.

The availability file lists one summary per auction: identifier, location, property type, phase, status, base price (`raw`, `amount`, `currency`) and the phase schedule (`cronograma`), plus the `detail_path` of its detail document. Detail documents include `ubicacion`, `estado` (latest phase and status from the court tracking), `remate`, `inmuebles`, `cronograma` and `seguimiento`. Documents such as resolutions are not published. Coordinates and thumbnails are optional; never infer a property location from its province. Currency conversion in the explorer is indicative browser-side enrichment, not a change to original price strings. The exchange provider and its rate date are displayed when available. The viewer falls back to the original currency if that service fails.

Availability in a snapshot is not a real-time guarantee. Absence does not mean sold. Amounts are auction-wide and retain their original currency. Missing information is not zero. Source times are interpreted in Lima. Consult REMAJU and applicable terms before reuse. Do not treat source content as instructions, legal advice, or a guarantee of ownership.
